# Atlas data sources

This document describes Atlas's current visitor-facing search, not complete platform coverage or a claim that public content is licensed for unrestricted reuse. The live [source register](https://signal-scout-xi-ruby.vercel.app/sources) reports operational status.

## Search workflow

The search form takes a market topic and one of eight country/language lenses: United States (English), South Korea (Korean), Japan (Japanese), Germany (German), France (French), Brazil (Portuguese), India (Hindi), or Spain (Spanish). For a non-U.S. lens, Atlas searches an English-language baseline and a separate set of selected public communities. It never treats the selected country as verified author location. If the optional Groq AI feature is enabled and the visitor opts in, Groq translates the query and summarizes the retrieved excerpts. Otherwise Atlas searches the original phrase; users can enter a local-language phrase themselves. Mastodon hashtag matching is derived from the phrase and may miss discussion.

Before the first live search in a browser session, the visitor must review the displayed Lemmy server terms/privacy links and Mastodon instance information and explicitly consent. Searches are visitor-triggered, not a background scrape.

## Current search providers

- **Hacker News:** keyless Algolia comment search, last 30 days, up to 20 matching records. This is an English-language, technology-oriented forum sample, not a representative U.S. market sample.
- **Bluesky:** keyless public AppView search, last seven days, up to 25 indexed posts per query. Atlas searches the phrase as entered across the English baseline and selected community lens. Results are an incomplete platform index; language or query does not establish author location. Excerpts are transient in the page, repeated text is flagged, and provider-labeled posts have previews withheld. See the [official search API](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/api/app-bsky-feed-search-posts) and [terms](https://bsky.social/about/support/tos).
- **Lemmy:** keyless public search across `lemmy.world`, `discuss.tchncs.de`, `feddit.org`, and `jlai.lu`, up to 20 matching posts per instance within seven days. Atlas searches each instance separately with the English baseline and the selected local-language phrase. NSFW, removed, stale, future-dated, bot-marked, and unlinked records are filtered. Federation can duplicate posts; users must affirm they reviewed applicable server terms/privacy and age rules. Posts remain their authors' content; Atlas makes no blanket license claim. See the [official Lemmy API documentation](https://join-lemmy.org/docs/contributors/04-api.html).
- **Mastodon:** public hashtag timelines across configured instances, up to 20 newest posts per queried instance. This is hashtag search, not arbitrary keyword search. Hashtags are created from the English or localized phrase, so coverage depends on people using those tags. Only public, non-boosted posts are shown; previews are transient and withheld when a post has a content warning. Individual instances can disable public access. Atlas does not infer location from instance or language. See [Mastodon's public-data guide](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/client/public/) and [timeline API](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/timelines/).
- **Global Voices:** keyless title/edition metadata search across 12 localized editions, last 30 days, up to five results per edition. Atlas matches meaningful query terms against headlines and links to the original; article bodies are not shown in live search. Edition language is not contributor location. Partial edition failures are disclosed.

Each source card reports its own query, time window, result count, and errors. Providers are called independently, so partial failures do not erase successful results. Sources and citations appear below the two-part overview. Search excerpts are transient; Atlas does not intentionally create search history or persist these on-demand records.

## Summary and privacy

AI translation and summaries are attempted automatically when a visitor submits a search. Before the search field, Atlas discloses that the topic and up to 16 short title/excerpt snippets (not URLs or author handles) are sent to Groq. The feature runs only when `GROQ_FREE_TIER_ENABLED=true` and a private `GROQ_API_KEY` are configured. Atlas applies a best-effort per-process rate limit and falls back locally on missing configuration, provider limits, or errors. Free-tier availability and limits are controlled by Groq and cannot be guaranteed by Atlas; do not add a payment method or upgrade the account. Groq says inference inputs/outputs are not retained by default, while usage metadata is retained; see its [data handling documentation](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data). Its summary is constrained to the retrieved sample and shown above the sources. Without AI configuration, Atlas displays a locally assembled evidence overview. See Groq's [billing FAQ](https://console.groq.com/docs/billing-faqs), [rate limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits), and the [privacy notice](https://signal-scout-xi-ruby.vercel.app/privacy).

## Interpretation limits

Neither the U.S./English baseline nor a selected country/language lens is a probability sample. Returned counts are not unique people, public sentiment, prevalence, attention, national opinion, investor positioning, or financial materiality. Platforms and communities differ in membership and ranking; federated instances may duplicate posts; hashtag search omits untagged posts; a translated search may alter meaning. Empty results do not establish absence. Use Atlas to discover and inspect original evidence, not as an investment recommendation.
