# Atlas data sources

This document describes Atlas's current visitor-facing search, not complete platform coverage or a claim that public content is licensed for unrestricted reuse. The live [source register](https://signal-scout-xi-ruby.vercel.app/sources) reports operational status.

Each overview reports the exact excerpt count and distinct platform labels that passed its filters. An empty or single-platform side is explicitly marked as insufficient to assess a cross-community view; multiple platform labels still do not establish independence, representativeness, or a like-for-like comparison.

## Search workflow

The search form takes a market topic and one of eight country/language lenses: United States (English), South Korea (Korean), Japan (Japanese), Germany (German), France (French), Brazil (Portuguese), India (Hindi), or Spain (Spanish). For a non-U.S. lens, Atlas searches a baseline and a separate set of selected public communities. Baseline language handling varies by provider and is not a verified U.S. sample. It never treats the selected country as verified author location. When configured, Groq automatically translates the query and summarizes only selected text-bearing excerpts; otherwise Atlas searches the original phrase and uses a deterministic, non-AI evidence overview. Users can provide a local-language phrase themselves. Mastodon hashtag matching is derived from the phrase and may miss discussion.

Short or ticker-like phrases trigger an ambiguity warning. A small explicit alias list can suggest a company/concept phrase; choosing it starts a new search with that exact phrase. Atlas never rewrites the submitted query silently. The alias list is intentionally incomplete: an unfamiliar or mixed-case ticker can still be ambiguous, so include a company, concept, or market in the query.

Searches are visitor-triggered, not a background scrape. There is no in-app terms-consent gate; the Sources page links to relevant instance policies, and visitors should review them. Providers or instances may restrict public access at any time.

## Current search providers

- **Bluesky:** keyless public AppView search, last seven days, up to 25 indexed posts per query. Atlas searches the phrase as entered across the English baseline and selected community lens. Results are an incomplete platform index; language or query does not establish author location. Excerpts are transient in the page, repeated text is flagged, and provider-labeled posts have previews withheld. Detectable external-link posts are flagged as link sharing: a linked headline or copied article is not necessarily the poster's independent view. Bluesky's terms reserve user content rights to the user and grant rights to Bluesky; public API access is not a blanket reuse license to Atlas. Atlas limits use to transient, attributed excerpts with original links; broader or commercial reuse remains subject to separate rights review. See the [official search API](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/api/app-bsky-feed-search-posts) and [terms](https://bsky.social/about/support/tos).
- **Lemmy:** keyless public search across `lemmy.world`, `discuss.tchncs.de`, `feddit.org`, `jlai.lu`, `sopuli.xyz`, and `sh.itjust.works`, up to 20 matching posts per instance within seven days. Atlas searches each instance separately with the English baseline and selected language phrase. Two newly added servers passed a live unauthenticated API check on 2026-10-11; availability is not guaranteed. NSFW, removed, stale, future-dated, bot-marked, and unlinked records are filtered. Detectable external-link posts are flagged as link sharing because linked headlines or copied reporting do not necessarily represent the poster's independent view. Federation can duplicate posts, and server/community views do not establish contributor location. Posts remain their authors' content; Atlas makes no blanket license claim. The Sources page links instance legal/privacy information. See the [official Lemmy API documentation](https://join-lemmy.org/docs/contributors/04-api.html).
- **Mastodon:** public hashtag timelines across configured instances, filtering posts older than 30 days, up to 20 API items per tag and up to three tags per instance. This is hashtag search, not arbitrary keyword search. Tags are queried separately, and coverage depends on people using them; broad tags can add noise. For a selected community-focused instance, Atlas passes the documented `local=true` filter to restrict results to statuses hosted locally; this says nothing about an author's nationality or residence. Other instances use the default timeline and may include federated statuses. Korean-, German-, French-, Brazilian Portuguese-, and Spanish-language/community-focused servers are selected for those lenses; no reviewed local-focused server is configured for India. Only public, non-boosted posts are shown; previews are transient and withheld when a post has a content warning. Detectable external-link posts are flagged as link sharing because linked headlines or copied reporting are not necessarily the poster's independent view. Individual instances can disable public access. See [Mastodon's public-data guide](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/client/public/), [timeline API](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/timelines/), and [official server directory](https://joinmastodon.org/servers).
- **Global Voices:** keyless title/edition metadata search across 12 localized editions, last 30 days, up to five results per edition. Atlas matches meaningful query terms against headlines and links to the original; article bodies are not shown in live search. Edition language is not contributor location. Partial edition failures are disclosed.
- **The Conversation:** visitor-triggered keyless title search across six first-party English Atom feeds (U.S., U.K., Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Africa), up to five exact headline matches per edition in 30 days. Feed rights are checked per item for Creative Commons attribution/no-derivatives; author, title, date, edition, and link are returned, while feed excerpts are discarded. This is expert analysis from one publisher, not public forum opinion or a geographic sample, and is excluded from the AI discussion synthesis.
- **Crossref:** one visitor-triggered request to the free public metadata API, without signup or key, returns up to 12 DOI-linked, five-year title matches. Atlas displays title, DOI, date, publisher/journal metadata, and a Crossref attribution link; it does not request abstracts or publisher full text. These are deposited scholarly records—not current discussion, investor opinion, country samples, or validated evidence quality. Crossref's [public API](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/) asks clients to cache repeated requests, stay within its public rate limits, identify their software, and back off when rate limited. Atlas reuses identical results in short-lived in-memory cache for 60 seconds, serializes distinct calls, applies a per-client request limit, and never writes the cache to persistent storage; serverless instances do not share it. Free-service uptime and shared-quota behavior cannot be guaranteed.
- **arXiv:** a public keyless Atom API search adds up to 12 five-year preprint title matches to global research context. The adapter discards abstracts and author names and keeps title, identifier, submission date, and subject category. arXiv's terms place descriptive metadata under CC0, while each paper's full-text rights vary; preprints are not necessarily peer-reviewed. API results change on a daily submission cycle, so Atlas uses a volatile per-instance 24-hour query cache and spaces distinct requests at least three seconds apart. Free-service uptime and coordination across serverless instances cannot be guaranteed. The source register displays arXiv's required acknowledgment.
- **Stack Exchange:** title-only advanced searches in Economics, Quantitative Finance, and Personal Finance & Money, plus Japanese, Portuguese, or Spanish Stack Overflow when the selected language has a corresponding site. Results are separately labeled expert Q&A, limited to individually CC BY-SA 4.0 questions with author/license attribution; bodies are not requested. Search is limited to recent matches and queries of 3–80 characters. Matching queries are reused for 65 seconds per browser session, API backoff is honored, and API-reported remaining quota is exposed when available. It is one specialist platform, not general community opinion. See [advanced search](https://api.stackexchange.com/docs/advanced-search), [throttling](https://api.stackexchange.com/docs/throttle), and [licensing](https://stackoverflow.com/help/licensing).
- **Reddit:** not integrated or scraped. Reddit's current [developer guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/14945211791892-Developer-Platform-Accessing-Reddit-Data) says research use must go through its separate Reddit for Researchers program; ordinary developer API access is not an authorized research route. Reconsider only if Atlas qualifies for and receives compatible authorization.

Each source card reports its own query, time window, retrieval time, provider-return count, topic-screened count, and availability/error status. It also reports how many unique excerpts passed the summary's content and language gates before platform/account balancing and the eight-excerpt cap; that is a candidate count, not proof the excerpts were all sent to AI or that they are relevant. Providers are called independently, so partial failures do not erase successful results. Sources and citations appear below the two-part overview. Search excerpts are transient; Atlas does not intentionally create search history or persist these on-demand records.

## Summary and privacy

AI translation and summaries are attempted automatically when a visitor submits a search. The topic and up to 16 short excerpts may be sent to Groq only when `GROQ_FREE_TIER_ENABLED=true` and a private `GROQ_API_KEY` are configured. For a named-language overview, only excerpts with an understood matching provider language label are eligible; missing, mismatched, and unresolved labels stay visible in results but are not accepted by the provider-label filter. Lemmy IDs are resolved from each post's own instance language catalog; unresolved IDs are never guessed, though conservative script-only fallback can include Korean, Japanese, or Hindi text when language metadata is unavailable. Embedded links, email addresses, and @handles are removed from excerpts. Social posts with a detectable external link remain visible in Sources but are excluded from AI evidence because linked headlines, previews, and promotions do not establish independent commentary; bare-domain detection is limited to common public suffixes, so shortened/hidden links and copied text without a detectable URL can pass. Citation URLs and attribution fields are not sent. Atlas applies a best-effort per-process rate limit and falls back locally on missing configuration, provider limits, or errors. Free-tier availability and limits are controlled by Groq and cannot be guaranteed by Atlas; do not add a payment method or upgrade the account. Groq says inference inputs/outputs are not retained by default, while usage metadata is retained; see its [data handling documentation](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data). Citation-ID validation confirms referenced IDs exist in the submitted group but does not verify semantic entailment. Citation links below each overview passage jump to the supporting records in the consolidated source register. The summary is constrained to the retrieved sample and shown above the sources. Without AI configuration, Atlas displays a locally assembled evidence overview. See Groq's [billing FAQ](https://console.groq.com/docs/billing-faqs), [rate limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits), and the [privacy notice](https://signal-scout-xi-ruby.vercel.app/privacy).

## Interpretation limits

Neither the U.S./English baseline nor a selected country/language lens is a probability sample. Crossref and arXiv records are global literature context only and never count as U.S. or selected-country community views. Returned counts are not unique people, public sentiment, prevalence, attention, national opinion, investor positioning, or financial materiality. Platforms and communities differ in membership and ranking; federated instances may duplicate posts; hashtag search omits untagged posts; a translated search may alter meaning. Empty results do not establish absence. Use Atlas to discover and inspect original evidence, not as an investment recommendation.

## Numeric claims in summaries

Every number in a generated claim must occur in the excerpt(s) cited for that claim. If a number from a social post is present but the model does not clearly attribute it, Atlas deterministically labels it as an unverified claim from the cited post(s). This only checks that the figure appears in the submitted text; it does not verify units, definitions, provenance, accuracy, or truth. Inspect the original post and any linked primary source before relying on a figure.
