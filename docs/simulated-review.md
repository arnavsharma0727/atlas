# Atlas simulated reviewer panel — 2026-10-12

This is an AI-authored simulation of three reviewer perspectives, requested by the operator. No human reviewer participated, no reviewer independence or language fluency is asserted, and no independent acceptance rows are generated. The roles share one reasoning system and codebase evidence. This document is a design review, not a benchmark, certification, investment recommendation, or 8/10 rating. Synthetic review may guide improvements without blocking personal use; it cannot turn missing empirical evidence into passing measurements.

## Perspective 1: fundamental researcher

Atlas can accelerate finding linked primary discussions across communities, but its useful output is a research starting point, not a verified thesis. A recently posted historical analogy must not become a current demand claim. Original opinions, issuer disclosures, forecasts, headlines and official statistics are different evidence types. Source diversity counts do not establish independent experience or causal support.

Disposition: usable for exploratory discovery with originals checked; unvalidated for decision-grade conclusions. Keep official datasets outside opinion summaries, show actual observation dates and units, retain contradictory material, and withhold unsupported or unavailable synthesis. Do not invent a U.S. population from English-language posts or infer country residence from a server/domain/language.

Action in this change: SEC is no longer a required source or operator setup task. Visitor and scheduled SEC access are disabled. Currency searches gain a dated ECB reference snapshot, explicitly excluded from AI opinion evidence. These changes do not solve uneven original-discussion breadth.

## Perspective 2: data-quality and provenance reviewer

The existing adapters expose bounded query windows, source status and original links; freshness gates and duplicate checks have synthetic regressions. Their limits matter: a short excerpt can omit context; numeric token agreement does not preserve a quantity's relationship; language/script matching is not fluent semantic review; the same-model grounding pass can repeat the generator's error.

Disposition: engineering safeguards are demonstrable, research precision/recall remains unmeasured. Do not fabricate sample sizes, inter-reviewer agreement or language qualifications. Real reviewer CSVs remain untouched. The ECB adapter validates dates, positive decimal values, missing/duplicate currencies and stale snapshots, retains unmodified values and attribution, and never converts a reference rate into an executable price or returns calculation. The shared snapshot cache preserves provider-fetch time instead of relabeling cache access as fresh retrieval.

## Perspective 3: skeptical personal-use researcher

The search-first interface matches the intended workflow: query, read a cautious overview when available, then inspect sources. It is more convenient than manually opening several communities only when it retrieves genuinely relevant, different original experiences. Thin or single-platform coverage should remain visible rather than being hidden behind a polished global narrative.

Disposition: appropriate to test privately, not grounds for an empirical quality score. A blocked AI request should leave linked results usable. Free access can fail or change; per-instance caching is not a globally coordinated traffic limit or an uptime guarantee. No payment, signup, new model provider or extra model calls are required by this change.

## Joint findings and unresolved limits

SEC setup is removed by explicit operator choice, not quietly scored as a successful SEC integration. ECB's latest working-day rates add timely official currency context; they do not add community opinions or national representation. Annual World Bank and monthly BIS series must retain their slower observation periods. Scholarly publication dates likewise do not prove that a paper measures current market conditions.

The panel cannot establish current multilingual precision, duplicate recall, summary semantic accuracy, independent source experience or national representativeness. The existing acceptance evaluator must continue to report insufficient evidence when its real worksheets are empty. No synthetic panel findings should be entered as human labels. Future permission-cleared original-discussion sources remain useful, but public reachability or free access alone is not a reuse license.
